POPIA Notice This policy is compiled in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa. As the Responsible Party, NamakwaChic is committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Who We Are

NamakwaChic is a South African fashion brand selling clothing and accessories online. When we refer to "we", "us" or "our", we mean NamakwaChic.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@namakwachic.co.za.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when necessary to provide our services. This includes:

  • Account & order information: your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address when you place an order.
  • Payment information: we do not store card details. Payments are processed securely by PayFast.
  • Communication: messages you send us via our contact form or email.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: your email address if you choose to subscribe.
  • Usage data: pages visited, time on site, and device information — collected anonymously via cookies if you consent.

3. Why We Collect Your Information

We process your personal information for the following lawful purposes:

  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To communicate with you about your orders and queries
  • To send you marketing emails if you have subscribed (you may opt out at any time)
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Delivery partners — to fulfil your order
  • PayFast — for secure payment processing
  • Firebase (Google) — our hosting and database provider
  • EmailJS — to send transactional emails

All third-party processors are required to handle your data securely and in accordance with applicable law.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to improve your browsing experience. When you first visit our site, we ask for your consent before placing non-essential cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies.

Essential cookies (required for the site to function) are always active.

6. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Order records are kept for 5 years for tax purposes. You may request deletion of your account data at any time (see Section 8).

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no online platform can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@namakwachic.co.za.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
